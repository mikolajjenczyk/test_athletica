## Executive Summary

**One-liner:** a web app where an academy coach sees the team's daily load and wellbeing, runs fitness tests on the pitch and approves AI readings of the data before the director sees them.

**Overview.** Football academy coaches keep test results, session load and wellbeing in spreadsheets, and join them by hand. Atletica reads the club's spreadsheets, takes test results typed on a phone or loaded from a file, and shows each player's own history first. An AI then writes a reading grounded in dated data. The coach accepts, corrects or rejects it. The director reads only what the coach approved.

For the first version, Apzumi frames the product as one loop: player data, an AI reading grounded in that data, the coach's decision (D230). The meeting of 21.10 moved the start of the coach's day to the team's daily data (session RPE, morning wellbeing). Tests now come below that (D285).

**Quick facts**
- **Target users:** the strength and conditioning coach (primary); the academy director and the club administrator (secondary).
- **Problem solved:** data spread across sheets, read by hand, with no shared history and no traceable conclusions.
- **Key metric:** the client's pilot targets (above 80% of tests done, above 70% of retests done, at least 70% of coaches active weekly, 4.5/5 satisfaction). The PRD also proposes a North Star metric; see Success Metrics.
- **Pilot:** 1–3 organisations, 50–300 athletes; one club, one team and one coach built per club (D08, D206).
- **Target launch:** not set. Technical specs stay parked until the client approves the brief (D360).

## Problem Statement

### The problem
A coach compares files, finds trends and writes reports for each audience by hand. Results lose value between seasons, and when the team or the coach changes. Each result is read without what came before. This is the client's description (pitch deck p.3). No coach other than the founder has described a real test day or report (A01, Q04).

### Current state
- Session RPE (each session's effort rating) and morning wellbeing are collected every day in shared spreadsheets that the players fill in. Tests come about every six weeks. This is the founder's account of 21.10 of general practice, not an observed week.
- 95% of practitioners who analyse test data use Excel (desk research, a survey of 102 practitioners; these are not the client's coaches).
- The client's benchmark sheet has 171 values from 26 publications, and none is approved for judging.

### Impact
- **Coach:** the time spent joining data is not measured (A01). The founder says the coach most needs to know who did not fill the sheet, and to catch risks first: overload, under-load, a drop in wellbeing or in the jump test.
- **Business:** the client wants a pilot and a case study on time saved and consistent retesting (deck p.30).

### Why now
The client sent its written notes and spreadsheets on 20.10 and set the direction with Apzumi on 21.10. A Polish-language client brief for scope approval is ready (`research/2026-10-21-brief-dla-klienta.md`).

## User Personas

The personas come from `research/personas.md`, which Tom confirmed on 21.10. They are role sketches built from the founder's words and synthetic panels. No real pilot coach has been heard.

### Primary persona: the coach who runs his team's tests himself
- **Context:** one team of 20–35 players, tests about every six weeks, training in the evening. He uses a phone outdoors (390 px wide, cold, gloves) and a laptop in the evening (assumed).
- **Needs:** see what is going on with the team today; know who needs a lighter load; get each test's best result in, then who improved or dropped; a clear next step.
- **Pain points:** a missing answer shown as zero or as fine; a conclusion he cannot trace to dated numbers; small fields on a cold pitch.
- **Will not accept:** an app that decides training for him.
- **Quote (the founder about this coach):** _„trener wchodzi i widzi informacje co się dzieje z drużyną na bieżąco”_
- **In the first version:** one coach enters the results and approves the reports (D206; Q07 is not confirmed by the client).

### Secondary personas
- **Academy director:** only reads, never enters or approves. His three questions: are the tests done, are retests on time, how are results changing. He sees no absence reasons, no wellbeing and no notes for the coach only. On the phone his menu has two items (D344).
- **Club administrator:** sets up the team, players, positions and invitations, on a computer only (D344).
- **Coach who struggles with technology** (edge case): small academy, stopwatch, three simple tests. Wants one obvious button and all Polish.

### People with no account in the first version
Players (children and teenagers) fill in the club's sheets and measure their height. Parents have no account either (N01, D237). The coach may show or print a reading to a player.

## User Stories & Requirements

**Priority.** P0 is the loop core and the acceptance scenario of D271. P1 is the rest of "Now". P2 is waiting for the client and drawn as a demo.

### Epic: Players' daily data

#### Story 1: Read the club's sheets (P0)
```
As a coach,
I want the app to read my team's RPE and wellbeing sheets on its own,
So that I start the day with current data and no upload work.
```
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] Given a shared team sheet, when the app reads it (automatically or on „Odśwież”), then it shows what it read before saving: values read, who misses which value on which days, and rejected rows with their number and reason (R22, D266).
- [ ] A row is matched to exactly one player and one session (player, date, session number) and is never saved twice; a changed value shows old and new and counts as a correction (D266).
- [ ] A missing RPE shows „Brak RPE” and a missing survey shows „Brak odpowiedzi”, never zero (R12).
- [ ] RPE uses 0–10, load = RPE × minutes (AU, arbitrary units), and two sessions a day stay apart (D334, D302).
- [ ] Wellbeing: of up to three surveys a day, the app counts the one marked morning or answered before 12:00, and the preview says so (D316, D330g). Six questions on the sheet's own scales, with sleep in hours (D353).
- [ ] Edge case: if the sheet cannot be read, a manual upload with the same preview is the fallback (D335).

**Effort:** L · **Dependencies:** where the sheets live and who grants access (Q81).

#### Story 2: See the team's day on the Pulpit (P0)
```
As a coach,
I want the Pulpit to open with the team's load and wellbeing and the players to check,
So that I know who needs attention before training.
```
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] Order from the top: title with date, a data-freshness line with „Odśwież”, „Test w trakcie · Wróć do testu” when a test is running, a one-line goal reminder, then the tiles „Obciążenie sesji (RPE)” and „Samopoczucie”, then „Zwróć uwagę”, then the „Testy” card, report and zalecenie links, and „Postęp drużyny” (D285, D370, D374, D389).
- [ ] Each „Zwróć uwagę” row says in plain words what is off, with a small chart; the whole row opens the player card on „Dziś” (D385, D378).
- [ ] When more than half of the players with data share a signal, one team risk replaces the rows for those players (D333).
- [ ] Risks come only from data, with numbers against the player's own level; never an injury forecast, a diagnosis or the word „niedotrenowany” (R24).

### Epic: Tests and results

#### Story 3: Run a test on the pitch (P0)
```
As a coach,
I want to enter a team's trials on my phone, one player at a time,
So that test day adds no work after training.
```
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] „Rozpocznij test” on a chosen test asks only for team or one player and the test date; earlier dates are allowed, future dates and dates before the player joined are not (D307, D162).
- [ ] „Tryb boisko”: a big keypad, reasons as big buttons, players in any order, „Dalej” to the next player with something missing (D310, D331).
- [ ] Without signal, results stay on the phone, show „niewysłane: N” and are sent later without double saving (D349).
- [ ] The result is the best trial, with its unit; a trial more than 10% off the last result or the other trials gets a light „sprawdź” mark, with no block (R02, D372).
- [ ] „Zakończ test” shows a summary (results, „Nie wykonał”, empty fields) and the next step (D202).

#### Story 4: Fix and complete results (P0)
```
As a coach,
I want to correct a result, add a late one or load past tests from a file,
So that the history is complete and honest.
```
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] „Edytuj wyniki” corrects in place, marked „poprawiony <data>”; a correction to an approved report triggers re-approval (D61, R18).
- [ ] „Dodaj wynik” offers „nadrobiony wynik” or „Test indywidualny”; an individual test never counts in team averages (D342, D113).
- [ ] A CSV of past results previews and rejects rows exactly as in Story 1 (D177, R22).
- [ ] Each kind of gap has its own state: empty, „Próba nieważna”, „Nie wykonał” with a reason, „Dołączył później”, „Brak wcześniejszego pomiaru” (R12).

#### Story 5: Know what is due (P1)
```
As a coach,
I want to see which tests are due and which results are missing,
So that retests happen on time.
```
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] Due date = test date + the test's „Powtarzaj co”; a passed date reads „termin był <data>” in amber with text, never red (D71, D169).
- [ ] „Zrealizowane: N z 6 · od <data>”, „Następne testy”, „Brakujące wyniki” and „Zmień termin” (old and new date side by side) (D251, D340, D257).
- [ ] Six active tests and twelve shown; each test shows its name with variant, unit, direction, attempts and which attempt counts; one method per test (R17, D213, D260).

### Epic: Player and team

#### Story 6: Read one player (P0)
```
As a coach,
I want a player's card to show today first and his own history before any comparison,
So that I judge him against himself.
```
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] The card opens on „Dziś”: goal, wellbeing, session load, height and growth rate, and „Obserwacje AI: N do sprawdzenia ›” (D378, D393).
- [ ] Reference points are named apart: own history, team average (with player count and date), „Cel” on a test, and an empty marked „Benchmark” place; never one good or bad verdict (R14, D186).
- [ ] Every number has a date and a base, and the direction is in words; charts space results by real dates (R15, D262).
- [ ] A test result is current until its next due date, and session RPE for 7 days; older data reads „Opisuje sytuację z <data>” (D268).
- [ ] A verdict appears only with a sourced „Wyraźna zmiana od”; otherwise the card shows the difference and „znaczenie zmiany wymaga oceny” (D259, D273).

#### Story 7: Read the team (P1)
```
As a coach,
I want one team page with its goals, players and readings,
So that I see the whole group in one place.
```
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] Tabs: Przegląd · Zawodnicy · Cele · Obserwacje AI · Zalecenia; on a computer, tabs that do not fit go into „Więcej ▾”; on the phone they scroll sideways (D328, D343).
- [ ] Przegląd: goal line, compact per-test rows, one two-line RPE and Samopoczucie chart (D391).
- [ ] Zawodnicy: a table on a computer; on the phone one line per player with six micro charts in test-set order (D391).

### Epic: AI readings and decisions

#### Story 8: Get an AI reading I can check (P0)
```
As a coach,
I want each AI observation to show the dated data it rests on,
So that I can trust it or reject it quickly.
```
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] Five parts: „Co zauważyliśmy”, „Na jakich danych”, „Co może to oznaczać”, „Co możesz rozważyć”, „Jak sprawdzimy”, plus „Czego brakuje” (D198).
- [ ] Each observation links to its dated records; values computed by the app, AI text and the coach's text are labelled apart, the PDF included (R21).
- [ ] The evidence cases of R20 apply: one result is a fact, two give a change, three or more a trend; sources that disagree are both named; one session's RPE, minutes and load are one proof (R20, R27).
- [ ] The AI reads „Plan sezonu”: in a planned tiring period, no change reads „utrzymany poziom” (D288, D336).
- [ ] The AI writes no exercises, loads, training directions or plans (R11, D240; see question 1 for Tom).
- [ ] Growth: at 7 cm or more in 12 months the AI may name „okres szybkiego wzrostu”, always with the numbers, never a maturation stage (R25).

#### Story 9: Decide on each observation (P0)
```
As a coach,
I want to accept, correct or reject each AI observation on its own,
So that only what I agree with moves on.
```
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] One „Przyjmij” per observation; „Popraw” opens an edit panel that saves with „Popraw i przyjmij”; „Odrzuć” has optional reasons (D375, D376, D366).
- [ ] An accepted observation is signed with date and source and can become a task („dla mnie” / „do przekazania zawodnikowi”, „dziś” / „w tym tygodniu”) (D292).
- [ ] A contradiction with a goal informs and never blocks approval (R16).
- [ ] The same state shows on the Pulpit and on the card (D366).

#### Story 10: Keep working when the AI fails (P0)
```
As a coach,
I want my work kept when the AI fails,
So that a report never depends on the AI.
```
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] On a timeout, an error or an answer without its basis, calculated values and the coach's text stay, and „Wygeneruj wnioski od nowa” is offered (D274).
- [ ] Generating again never touches the coach's own text, and asks before replacing AI text he edited.
- [ ] A report written without AI carries no AI label.

### Epic: Reports and director

#### Story 11: Approve a report for the director (P0)
```
As a coach,
I want to approve a player or team report and see exactly what the director will get,
So that nothing reaches him unchecked.
```
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] The draft shows conclusions first and charts below, labelled „Wnioski robocze do sprawdzenia” (D377, D255).
- [ ] „Zapisz szkic” keeps it „Niewidoczny dla dyrektora”; „Zatwierdź i pokaż dyrektorowi” publishes it; „Usuń szkic” is quiet (D111, D216).
- [ ] AI text the director can read uses only data he may see: no gap reasons, wellbeing, players' notes or coach-only notes (R19).
- [ ] After a data change the report reads „Wymaga ponownego zatwierdzenia”, lists what to re-read and has no PDF until approved again (R18).
- [ ] An approved report downloads as a PDF that opens with a short summary (D173).
- [ ] The coach can approve on the phone (D349).

#### Story 12: See the team's status as director (P1)
```
As a director,
I want the same counters as the coach and the approved reports,
So that I know whether testing is on track and who is responsible.
```
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] The counters use the same definitions as the coach's; the responsible coach is named at overdue items (D190, D203).
- [ ] No absence reasons, wellbeing or coach's free text anywhere, also not in AI text (D109, D345, D265).
- [ ] The Raporty list shows „Utworzono”, „Zatwierdzono” and „Dane do” (D396).

### Epic: Goals, zalecenia, club

#### Story 13: Set goals and a season plan (P1)
```
As a coach,
I want to write the team's goals and a simple season plan,
So that the AI reads results against my intentions.
```
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] „Cel” on a test (value, deadline, author, reason); „Cel drużyny” and „Cel zawodnika” the coach ticks himself (D223, D226).
- [ ] „Plan sezonu”: season goal with a date, key events and an optional typical week, in plain editable text; no month-by-month plan (D336).

#### Story 14: Give and review a zalecenie (P1)
```
As a coach,
I want to give a recommendation linked to tests and rate it later,
So that I learn what worked.
```
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] Each test in a zalecenie shows its own state; one review, „Ocena realizacji i efektu zalecenia”, in three parts; „Jeszcze nie można ocenić” keeps it open (D200, D246, D270).
- [ ] The director sees tests, dates, state and the review choice, not the coach's text (D265).

#### Story 15: Set up the club (P1)
```
As a club administrator,
I want to invite people and set up the team,
So that the coach can start.
```
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] Accounts are created by invitation only; „Status konta” shows pending invitations (R07, D204).
- [ ] Deactivating an account removes access at once; a rough club-data deletion flow ends with a confirmation (R26, D352).
- [ ] Clubs never see each other's data (D33); „Ukryj nazwiska” hides every person's name (D184).

#### P2 stories: waiting for the client, drawn as demos
- „Test gotowości (CMJ)”: a quick jump check outside the six tests (D309, D394; Q80).
- „Notatki”: notes dictated with AI speech recognition, audio not kept (D294, D365; Q84).
- „Obserwacje AI” lines on the Pulpit, each with at least two sources (D354, D366; Q97).
- „Zapytaj dane”: an AI chat demo (D395; the real feature comes later, D295).

## Success Metrics

### Primary metric (North Star), a proposal of this PRD
**Metric:** coach decisions on current data per active coach per week. A decision is an AI observation accepted, corrected, rejected or turned into a task.
**Why:** it counts the loop of D230 (data, reading, decision), not screen visits.
**Baseline:** none. **Target:** set with the client after the first pilot weeks. This metric is not in any decision; Tom decides whether to keep it.

### Secondary metrics: the client's pilot targets (deck p.30)

| Metric | Current | Target | Timeframe |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tests completed | no baseline | above 80% | pilot |
| Retests completed | no baseline | above 70% | pilot |
| Weekly active coaches | no baseline | at least 70% | pilot |
| Satisfaction | no baseline | 4.5 of 5 | end of pilot |
| Report preparation time | not measured | lower | pilot |

"Active" and "completed" are not defined yet. Whether the app must record these figures itself is open (Q29).

### Guardrails (target: 0 cases)
- A gap counted as zero or as a good score (R12).
- A file row matched by guess or saved twice (R22).
- An absence reason, wellbeing answer or coach-only note in text the director reads (R19).
- A report counted as approved after its data changed (R18).
- A new admin task for the coach on test day (deck p.11).

### Measurement framework
HEART (happiness, engagement, adoption, retention, task success):
- **Adoption:** 70% weekly active coaches.
- **Engagement:** the Pulpit opened on 4 or more days a week. This checks A11, the assumption that daily data brings the coach in every day.
- **Task success:** above 80% of tests done, above 70% of retests done.
- **Happiness:** 4.5 of 5.
- **Retention:** the club still uploads RPE in week 6 of the pilot.

**Events to track (if Q29 is yes):** `sheet_read`, `test_finished`, `observation_decided`, `report_approved`, `pulpit_opened`.

## Scope

### In scope

**Phase 1, the first version ("Now", D242, D271):**
- One club, one team, one coach built; more teams shown and marked „po MVP” (D206).
- Session RPE and morning wellbeing, read from the club's sheet (Stories 1–2).
- Six active tests: entry, „Tryb boisko”, corrections, CSV of past results, due dates (Stories 3–5).
- Player card and team page (Stories 6–7).
- „Obserwacje AI” with a decision per observation (Stories 8–10).
- Player and team reports with approval and PDF; the director's view (Stories 11–12).
- Goals, a simple „Plan sezonu”, zalecenia, club setup (Stories 13–15).

**Waiting for the client (drawn as demos):** „Test gotowości (CMJ)”, „Notatki” with dictation, Pulpit AI lines, goals × events, a players' link for height, team or position zalecenia (Q80, Q83, Q84, Q88, Q97, D347).

**Phase 2 (after the first version, „po MVP”):** player form; „Zapytaj dane”; the club reference library and external benchmarks; Atletica's knowledge base for the AI; several teams and a club view; choosing a report period; configuring the test set; several methods per test; a readiness value; device integrations.

### Out of scope
- Injury or talent prediction: it needs a dataset and validation (N05).
- Medical data, diagnoses, a maturation stage, device-measured sleep or HRV (heart rate variability), a pain map (N17).
- Pain, symptoms, „Kontakt?” and the notes column of the wellbeing sheet (N18).
- AI training content in the first version (N11, R11).
- Player and parent accounts (N01).
- Formal security policies during the pilot: retention schedules, field-level rules, change logs (D352).

## Technical Considerations

- Open Mercato app in `app/`, module `src/modules/athletes/`. On main: teams, players, the test set, three club roles and the club guard (phase 1).
- Two draft PRs, #5 (test day backend) and #6 (Karta zawodnika), were built on the model of 9.10. Later decisions replaced parts of it: no reopen, no fixed 42-day retest, read-only thresholds (D60, D71, D99). Both need rework before any merge.

### Data model
Club, Team, Player, TestDefinition (with threshold and its source), TestSession, Trial, Absence, Result, DueDate. Still to specify: Report, Zalecenie, Goal, Plan sezonu, RPE session, Wellbeing survey, Height, Note, AI observation state, Task.

### Integrations
- **Club spreadsheet:** automatic read (likely Google Drive; Q81), with manual upload as the fallback.
- **AI provider:** EU only; not chosen (Q17).
- **Speech recognition:** a Whisper-class model with player names as vocabulary; where it runs is open (D365).
- **PDF renderer:** not chosen.

### Security
- Invite-only accounts and per-club scope. Today an operator sets each user's club by hand (D33); invitations by the club administrator need a fix (conflict log, C20).
- Instant deactivation and a rough deletion flow; encryption of players' personal data (R26).

### Scale
Pilot: 1–3 clubs, about 25 players a team, 50–300 athletes. No load targets are set; see NFR-010.

## Design & UX Requirements

### UX principles
- Daily data first, tests below (D285).
- Own history before comparisons (D185).
- A gap is never zero, and every number has a date (R12, R15).
- Every text says who reads it (R13).
- Light and simple, in the founder's words: _„atletika ma być trenerem”_ ("Atletica should be a coach").

### Primary flow (acceptance scenario, D271)
1. The coach loads files with gaps and one unreadable row; the preview names the gaps and the rejected row.
2. He reads one player's AI reading, with its dated basis.
3. He accepts or corrects it, then creates and approves a player report.
4. The director opens the report and downloads the PDF.

### Alternative flows
- **Morning:** Pulpit → „Zwróć uwagę” → player card „Dziś” → decision → task.
- **Pitch:** „Rozpocznij test” → „Tryb boisko” → „Zakończ test”.
- **AI failure:** the coach writes the text himself; the report carries no AI label.

### Visual design
- Lo-fi prototype: `prototype/index.html` (rev-051) on the Apzumi Design System V2.9.5 as it is: Lato, type scale 28/20/15/13, cards with radius 18 (D382, D386, D387).
- One mini-chart component everywhere: the last 7 days, the newest value at the right edge (D397).
- ⓘ help is short bullet lists (D388). Colour is always paired with text.
- The hi-fi layer (gold, green, turquoise, warning colours) is a later design task (D298).

### Accessibility
